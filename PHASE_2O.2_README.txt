ManaChaiLeasing - Phase 2O.2
Overdue Item Sale Workflow

Version:
0.3.6

What changed:
- Adds Sale as a financial transaction type.
- Adds Sold as a final pawn-ticket status.
- Sale is available only for an Active ticket whose current due date has passed.
- The operator records sale date, actual sale amount, payment method, and optional note.
- Saving a sale creates an Income transaction and changes the ticket to Sold.
- A sold ticket cannot be renewed, redeemed, or sold again.
- Automatic Backup runs after a successful sale.

Dashboard:
- Adds Sale count and Sale income for Today / This Week / This Month / Custom.
- Total Income and Net Cash include Sale income.
- Profit includes Sale profit or loss.

Profit formula:
- Interest-renewal income.
- Plus final interest from Redemption.
- Plus Sale amount minus original pawn principal.

Example:
- Principal: 10,000.00
- Sale amount: 13,000.00
- Sale profit: 3,000.00

Search and history:
- Adds the Sold search filter.
- Search results show Sold status.
- Ticket history and Today's Transactions show Sale transactions.

Database:
- No migration and no schema change.
- Enum values are stored as text in the existing columns.

Core UAT:
1. Create an overdue Active pawn ticket.
2. Open ticket detail; Sale Item must be enabled.
3. Enter sale date, amount, payment method, and save.
4. Confirm status becomes Sold and history shows Sale / Income.
5. Confirm Renew, Redeem, and Sale buttons are disabled afterward.
6. Confirm Search > Sold finds the ticket.
7. Confirm Dashboard Sale, Total Income, Net Cash, and Profit are correct.
8. Confirm Today's Transactions shows the Sale when sale date is today.
